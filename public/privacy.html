Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

JepongDevxyz AllDL is a free tool with no account and no sign-in. This policy explains in plain language what happens to information when you use it.

1. Information we do NOT collect

We do not ask for, and do not collect, your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information. There is no account, no login, and no profile. We do not sell any information, because we do not collect any.

2. Links you paste

When you paste a video link and tap Generate, that link is sent to our server so it can be resolved into a download link. The link is forwarded to the service that handles that platform — YouTube links go through a video-conversion API, TikTok links go through a TikTok resolver service, and Facebook links go through our video-extraction backend — and the resulting download link is returned to you. We do not store the links you paste. They exist only while your request is being processed. Please do not paste private or sensitive links.

3. Anonymous download counts

We keep a simple anonymous counter of how many downloads have been generated, shown separately per platform (YouTube, TikTok, Facebook) on the main page. This counter contains only numbers — no links, no IP addresses, and nothing that identifies you.

4. On-device preference

Your dark/light theme choice is saved only on your own device (in your browser's local storage) so the site remembers it next time. It is never sent to our server.

5. Cookies and tracking

AllDL itself does not use tracking cookies, advertising trackers, or analytics that identify you. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (for example, IP addresses and request times) for security and reliability, as most hosting providers do. We do not use those logs to identify individual users.

6. Third-party services

To generate download links, the Service relies on third-party services and the original platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Facebook). When you use AllDL, your pasted link and standard request information may be visible to those services under their own privacy policies. Downloading a file may also connect your browser directly to the video host (for example, a video CDN) to fetch the file. We do not control those third parties.

7. Children's privacy

AllDL is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Minors should use the Service only with a parent or guardian's permission.

8. Data retention and your choices

Because we do not store your links or personal information, there is nothing to delete on our side. You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to remove the saved theme preference. If you do not want a link to be sent to our server and the third-party services above, simply do not paste it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do. Continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the site owner through JepongDevxyz.
